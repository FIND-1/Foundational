<h1>Python3 数据类型转换</h1>
<p>有时候，我们需要对数据内置的类型进行转换，数据类型的转换，一般情况下你只需要将数据类型作为函数名即可。</p>

<p>Python 数据类型转换可以分为两种：</p>
<ul><li>
隐式类型转换 - 自动完成</li><li>
显式类型转换 - 需要使用类型函数来转换</li></ul>

<h3>隐式类型转换</h3>
<p>在隐式类型转换中，Python 会自动将一种数据类型转换为另一种数据类型，不需要我们去干预。</p>


<p>
以下实例中，我们对两种不同类型的数据进行运算，较低数据类型（整数）就会转换为较高数据类型（浮点数）以避免数据丢失。</p>
<div class="example"><h2 class="example">实例</h2> <div class="example_code">
num_int <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Maroon;">123</span><br />
num_flo <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Maroon;">1.23</span><br />
<br />
num_new <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> num_int + num_flo<br />
<br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_int 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_int<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_flo 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_flo<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_new 值为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span>num_new<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_new 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_new<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
</div></div>

<p>以上实例输出结果为：</p>
<pre>num_int 数据类型为: &lt;class 'int'&gt;
num_flo 数据类型为: &lt;class 'float'&gt;
num_new: 值为: 124.23
num_new 数据类型为: &lt;class 'float'&gt;</pre>
<p>代码解析：</p>
<ul><li>实例中我们对两个不同数据类型的变量 <code>num_int</code> 和 <code>num_flo</code> 进行相加运算，并存储在变量 <code>num_new</code> 中。</li>
<li>然后查看三个变量的数据类型。</li>
<li>在输出结果中，我们看到 <code>num_int</code> 是 <code>整型（integer）</code> ， <code>num_flo</code> 是 <code> 浮点型（float）</code>。</li>
<li>同样，新的变量 <code>num_new</code> 是 <code> 浮点型（float）</code>，这是因为 Python 会将较小的数据类型转换为较大的数据类型，以避免数据丢失。</li>
</ul>
<p>我们再看一个实例，整型数据与字符串类型的数据进行相加：</p>

<div class="example"><h2 class="example">实例</h2> <div class="example_code">
num_int <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Maroon;">123</span><br />
num_str <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: #a11;">&quot;456&quot;</span><br />
<br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_int 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_int<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_str 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_str<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_int+num_str<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
</div></div>
<p>以上实例输出结果为：</p>

<pre>num_int 数据类型为: &lt;class 'int'&gt;
num_str 数据类型为: &lt;class 'str'&gt;
Traceback (most recent call last):
File "/runoob-test/test.py", line 7, in &lt;module&gt;
print(num_int+num_str)
TypeError: unsupported operand type(s) for +: 'int' and 'str'</pre>

<p>从输出中可以看出，整型和字符串类型运算结果会报错，输出 TypeError。 Python 在这种情况下无法使用隐式转换。</p>
<p>但是，Python 为这些类型的情况提供了一种解决方案，称为显式转换。</p>

<h3>显式类型转换</h3>

<p>在显式类型转换中，用户将对象的数据类型转换为所需的数据类型。 我们使用 int()、float()、str() 等预定义函数来执行显式类型转换。</p>

<p><span class="marked">int()</span> 强制转换为整型：</p>
<div class="example"><h2 class="example">实例</h2> <div class="example_code">
x <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">int</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: Maroon;">1</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> &nbsp; <span style="color: #a50"># x 输出结果为 1</span><br />
y <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">int</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: Maroon;">2.8</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> <span style="color: #a50"># y 输出结果为 2</span><br />
z <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">int</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;3&quot;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> <span style="color: #a50"># z 输出结果为 3</span><br />
</div></div>

<p><span class="marked">float()</span> 强制转换为浮点型：</p>
<div class="example"><h2 class="example">实例</h2> <div class="example_code">
x <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">float</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: Maroon;">1</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> &nbsp; &nbsp; <span style="color: #a50"># x 输出结果为 1.0</span><br />
y <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">float</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: Maroon;">2.8</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> &nbsp; <span style="color: #a50"># y 输出结果为 2.8</span><br />
z <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">float</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;3&quot;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> &nbsp; <span style="color: #a50"># z 输出结果为 3.0</span><br />
w <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">float</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;4.2&quot;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> <span style="color: #a50"># w 输出结果为 4.2</span><br />
</div></div>

<p><span class="marked">str()</span> 强制转换为字符串类型：</p>
<div class="example"><h2 class="example">实例</h2> <div class="example_code">
x <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">str</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;s1&quot;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> <span style="color: #a50"># x 输出结果为 's1'</span><br />
y <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">str</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: Maroon;">2</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> &nbsp; &nbsp;<span style="color: #a50"># y 输出结果为 '2'</span><br />
z <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">str</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: Maroon;">3.0</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> &nbsp;<span style="color: #a50"># z 输出结果为 '3.0'</span><br />
</div></div>

<p>整型和字符串类型进行运算，就可以用强制类型转换来完成：</p>

<div class="example"><h2 class="example">实例</h2> <div class="example_code">
num_int <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Maroon;">123</span><br />
num_str <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: #a11;">&quot;456&quot;</span><br />
<br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_int 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_int<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;类型转换前，num_str 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_str<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<br />
num_str <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> <span style="color: Teal;">int</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_str<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span> &nbsp; &nbsp;<span style="color: #a50"># 强制转换为整型</span><br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;类型转换后，num_str 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_str<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<br />
num_sum <span style="color: Gray;">=</span> num_int + num_str<br />
<br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;num_int 与 num_str 相加结果为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span>num_sum<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
<span style="color: Green;font-weight:bold;">print</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span><span style="color: #a11;">&quot;sum 数据类型为:&quot;</span><span style="color: Gray;">,</span><span style="color: Teal;">type</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#40;</span>num_sum<span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><span style="color: Olive;">&#41;</span><br />
</div></div>

<p>以上实例输出结果为：</p>

<pre>num_int 数据类型为: &lt;class 'int'&gt;
类型转换前，num_str 数据类型为: &lt;class 'str'&gt;
类型转换后，num_str 数据类型为: &lt;class 'int'&gt;
num_int 与 num_str 相加结果为: 579
sum 数据类型为: &lt;class 'int'&gt;</pre>
